Getting there
& where to stay

Flights

Fly into Heraklion

Heraklion airport (HER) is the closest, with plenty of direct flights from UK airports in late May. Lyttos Beach is about 35 minutes east along the coast by taxi or private transfer.

Add your arrival date to your RSVP and we'll pair you up with others landing around the same time.
